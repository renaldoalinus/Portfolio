## The context

Homeowners who wanted to finance a solar installation faced a fragmented,
paper-heavy process: multiple PDFs, manual credit checks, and days of
back-and-forth between the installer, the bank, and the customer. Drop-off
was high and installers were losing deals to slow approvals.

## The problem

> How might we let a homeowner go from "interested" to "financing approved"
> in a single sitting — without sacrificing the trust a financial decision demands?

## My role

I led design end-to-end: research synthesis, information architecture,
interaction design, prototyping, and hand-off. I partnered with two engineers,
a product manager, and our risk team.

## What I did

- Mapped the existing 14-step journey and identified the three moments where
  most people abandoned.
- Reframed the flow around a single continuous conversation rather than a
  series of forms.
- Designed a progressive-disclosure pattern so heavy financial detail only
  appeared when it mattered.
- Prototyped and tested three approval-status models with 8 homeowners.

## The outcome

The redesigned flow cut time-to-approval from roughly five days to under
twenty minutes for eligible customers, and lifted completion of the financing
step significantly. Installers began using the flow live, in the customer's
living room, as a closing tool.
