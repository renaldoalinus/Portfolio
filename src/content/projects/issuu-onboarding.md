## Overview

Issuu is a publishing platform used by a wide range of users across B2B and B2C, spanning multiple industries, company sizes, and levels of product knowledge.

This project focused on redesigning Issuu’s onboarding experience to help new users understand the platform’s value earlier, by tailoring the experience based on their goals, context, and familiarity with Issuu.

The outcome was a personalized onboarding flow that increased activation and conversion by guiding users to the most relevant features for them, as early as possible.


## My Role

I was responsible for:

- User research (interviews, desk research, competitive analysis)

- Synthesizing insights and validating the concept

- UX and UI design of the onboarding flow

- Hand-drawing and designing the illustrations used in the experience

I worked closely with product and design leadership throughout ideation, validation, and experimentation.


## The Problem

Issuu offers a broad and powerful set of features, but many new users only discovered its core value later in their journey, after already investing time and effort.

From previous research and internal insights, we identified several recurring issues:

- New users were unsure which features were relevant to them

- The product felt overwhelming, especially for first-time users

- Users often explored features that were not aligned with their primary goal

- Value realization happened too late, impacting activation and conversion

Problem statement:

"How might we offer a more personal, tailored onboarding experience that helps users quickly understand Issuu’s value and activate them earlier, despite their very different goals, backgrounds, and use cases?"


## Research & Insights

Issuu does not use traditional personas, but instead works with Archetypes, which represent recurring patterns of user needs and behaviors.

To inform the solution, I conducted and analyzed:

- User interviews

- Desk research

- Review of past qualitative research (Dovetail insights)

- Competitive analysis of onboarding patterns in adjacent products

A key insight consistently emerged:

Users didn’t know which features mattered most to them, or how to find them quickly.

This lack of guidance created friction and delayed value discovery — especially problematic given Issuu’s wide feature set.


## The Approach

Instead of presenting the same onboarding experience to everyone, we designed a flow that adapts to the user based on a short set of questions at the beginning.

The onboarding flow is structured in two main parts:

- Intent-setting questions

- Tailored in-product guidance through tooltips


## Step 1: Understanding User Intent

At the start of onboarding, users are asked a small number of questions to understand their context:

- What is your primary goal on Issuu?

- How familiar are you with Issuu?

- What best describes your work or industry?

- How large is your organization?

Each answer maps to one or more Issuu Archetypes, allowing us to infer:

- Which features are most valuable

- Which actions should be prioritized

- How much guidance the user needs

This step helps the product adapt before users interact with complex functionality.

## Step 2: Personalized Tooltips & Guidance

Based on the answers given, users are then guided through Issuu using contextual tooltips.

These tooltips:

- Highlight only the most relevant actions for that user

- Encourage users to complete tasks that demonstrate value early

- Reduce cognitive overload by avoiding unnecessary features

Examples include:

- Uploading a first document

- Publishing and sharing a flipbook

- Embedding content

- Creating supporting assets such as social posts or articles

The guidance is intentionally lightweight, designed to support discovery without interrupting the core workflow.

## A Conscious Scope Decision

An early concept included a gamified onboarding checklist that tracked progress across multiple tasks.

After validation and internal discussions, we decided to remove this for the first version, because:

- It added too much complexity early on

- The concept was not fully thought through yet

- We wanted to validate the core personalization logic first

Instead, we focused on high-impact, contextual guidance through tooltips, leaving room to evolve the checklist concept in future iterations.


![](/work/issuu-onboarding/08.png)


## Validation & Experimentation

The concept was:

- User-tested with newly signed-up Issuu users

- Iterated to improve clarity, pacing, and tone

- Enhanced visually to make the experience more enjoyable and memorable

Once aligned with stakeholders, we ran an A/B test comparing the personalized onboarding flow against the existing experience.


## Results

- Variant 1 increased conversion by over 5%

- Users reached key activation moments faster

- Early feedback indicated improved clarity and reduced confusion

- The approach validated personalization as a strong lever for activation


## Learnings

- Early value discovery is critical in feature-rich products

- Personalization doesn’t need to be complex to be effective

- Lightweight, contextual guidance can outperform heavier onboarding mechanics

- Iteration and scope control are key to shipping impactful solutions
